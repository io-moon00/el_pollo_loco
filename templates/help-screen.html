El Pollo Loco – Help

Welcome to the wild lands of El Pollo Loco! Pepe, the brave adventurer, is on a mission to find the legendary Salsa Recipe – but the feathered locals aren’t too happy about it.

Angry chickens and sneaky chicks will try to stop him at every step. Collect Salsa bottles to prepare for the ultimate showdown against El Pollo Diablo, the fiery lord of all chickens! Don’t forget to grab coins along the way – they’ll boost your score and prove your worth as the true master of the chicken realm.

Dodge attacks, jump over obstacles, and keep moving – because in El Pollo Loco, only the spiciest heroes survive! 🌶️🐔

How to Play

  • or D — Move forward
  • or A — Move backward
  • or W — Jump
  • Q — Throw a Salsa Bottle

Master these moves, collect your salsa, and face El Pollo Diablo with style! 🌶️🔥
